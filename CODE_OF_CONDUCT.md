Geoff-Sim welcomes constructive participation from people with different
backgrounds, experience levels and perspectives. This policy applies to project
issues, pull requests, discussions and other spaces where participants represent
the project.

## Expected conduct

- Treat people respectfully and critique ideas, code and evidence rather than individuals.
- Make room for questions, accessibility needs and different levels of experience.
- Give credit for contributions and preserve source attribution.
- Report results honestly, including failures, uncertainty and limitations.
- Respect privacy and avoid sharing another person's private information.
- Follow maintainer requests intended to keep collaboration productive.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized conduct, deliberate intimidation,
personal attacks and publication of private information are unacceptable.
Repeated disruptive behavior after a request to stop is also unacceptable.

## Reporting and response

For a conduct concern, contact the maintainer privately using the contact details
in the [project profile](profile/README.md). Include the relevant links or dates
and enough context to understand the concern. Share sensitive details privately,
rather than adding them to a public issue.

Maintainers may clarify expectations, request changes, remove inappropriate
content, or restrict participation in response to the circumstances and repeated
behavior. Reports should be handled discreetly and conflicts of interest disclosed.
If the concern involves the maintainer, GitHub's
[abuse-reporting process](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam)
is an additional route for conduct on GitHub.

For vulnerabilities, use [Security reporting](SECURITY.md#reporting-a-vulnerability).
For technical contributions, see [Contributing](CONTRIBUTING.md).
